# Neon Drift

**Enter the neon. Collect the energy. Dodge the void. Keep drifting.**

Neon Drift is a fast-paced, neon-soaked arcade survival game where every mission pushes your reflexes further. Guide your glowing ship through shifting futuristic environments, collect energy crystals, and dodge dangerous red hazard shards. The longer you survive, the faster and more intense the run becomes.

Play in your browser with simple mouse or touch controls. Complete each energy objective to unlock the next mission, explore new environments, and see how far you can drift.

## How to play

- **Steer:** Move your pointer or finger horizontally to guide your ship.
- **Collect energy:** Gather glowing energy crystals to fill your mission objective.
- **Avoid hazards:** Dodge red hazard shards. One bad move can end your run.
- **Complete missions:** Reach the required energy target to advance.
- **Beat your best:** Build your score, chase your high score, and keep pushing into tougher missions.

## Missions and environments

The game gets harder as you progress. Each mission increases the energy target, while hazards become faster and appear more frequently.

| Missions | Environment | Challenge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | **Neon Highway** | Learn the controls and collect energy through a cool cyan-and-blue neon landscape. |
| 3–4 | **Industrial Tunnel** | Navigate a magenta-and-purple tunnel with glowing structural ribs and tighter pressure. |
| 5+ | **Red Void** | Enter a red-and-orange danger zone with pulsing warning lights and a much more intense pace. |

The mission sequence is designed to keep raising the stakes: collect more energy, react faster, and stay alive as the world changes around you.

## Features

- Fast, accessible arcade gameplay in a browser
- Mouse and touch-friendly steering
- Progressive missions with increasing energy targets
- Three distinct neon environments that change as missions advance
- Dynamic difficulty with faster hazards and more frequent spawns
- Glowing energy pickups and dangerous hazard shards
- Animated perspective grid, neon rails, and environment-specific effects
- Score tracking and best-score chasing
- Synth-style sound, visual particles, and feedback effects
- Shareable scores
- Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework or build step required

## Controls

**Mouse:** Move the pointer to steer.

**Touch:** Drag or move your finger to steer on a touchscreen.

Collect the energy, avoid the red hazards, and complete the objective shown in the mission HUD.

## Run locally

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern browser, or serve the folder with any local static server.
3. Start drifting.

No package installation or build command is required.
